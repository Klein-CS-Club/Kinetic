01 / 09　·　NEPTUNE

Set the boundary.

Create a universe for one team or business. Its projects, deadlines, and staffing forecasts stay together and are saved in this browser.

02 / 09　·　URANUS

Group related projects.

Use a galaxy for a standalone project. When several projects share a program or client, put their galaxies in one cluster.

03 / 09　·　SATURN

Divide the work.

Open a galaxy and add a stellar system for each workstream. Design and Development can share a project while keeping separate milestones.

04 / 09　·　JUPITER

Make priorities visible.

Give an object Normal, High, or Critical priority. Its size changes on the map, so the team can spot important work before opening the details.

05 / 09　·　MARS

Plan a milestone.

Add a planet inside a stellar system, then add moons for its tasks. A launch milestone might contain final QA, approval, and release.

06 / 09　·　EARTH

Assign the work.

Record an owner, deadline, and effort in person-days. Update the status as work moves from planned to in progress to done.

07 / 09　·　VENUS

Sequence the handoffs.

In Planning details, use Starts after to choose a prerequisite. The forecast schedules the dependent task after that prerequisite finishes.

08 / 09　·　MERCURY

Compare finish dates.

Open Workspace tools and compare staffing. Change the number of available workers to see whether parallel tasks can finish sooner.

09 / 09　·　SUN

Create your first galaxy.

Enter the workspace, name a universe, and add a galaxy for your first project. A short optional tour shows how to arrange and edit its objects.
